Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2025 — Abrarr Tallks | rebrar.in | MBP

📅 Effective Date: 1 September 2025  |  Applies to: Services by Abrar Ali via rebrar.in & MBP  |  Contact: +91 7051078832

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all web design, web development and SEO services provided by Abrar Ali (Abrarr Tallks), CEO of rebrar.in and MBP (My Business Promotion), based in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India.

We believe in transparent, fair dealing. Please read this policy carefully before placing any order or making any payment.

1. General Refund Principles

All web design and development services involve significant time, effort and skill. Once work has commenced on your project, certain costs cannot be recovered. Our refund policy reflects this reality while remaining fair to clients.

2. Refund Eligibility by Project Stage

3. Non-Refundable Items

The following are non-refundable under any circumstances:

  • Domain registration fees (paid to third-party registrars)
  • Hosting fees paid to third-party hosting providers
  • Third-party plugin or software licences purchased for your project
  • Rush/express project fees (projects with accelerated timelines)
  • Any completed milestone payments that have been approved by the client

4. Revision Policy

Before requesting a refund, we encourage clients to first make use of included revisions. All packages include 2–3 rounds of free revisions. Additional revisions beyond the package allowance are available at ₹500–₹1,500 per revision round depending on scope.

Revision requests must be submitted within 14 days of project delivery. Requests after this period may be treated as a new project.

5. Cancellation Process

To cancel a project or request a refund:

  • Send a cancellation request via WhatsApp to +91 7051078832 or email abrar@abrartallks-portfolio.co.in
  • Include your name, project details and reason for cancellation
  • We will acknowledge within 24 business hours and process any eligible refund within 7–10 business days

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed via the original payment method:

  • Bank Transfer / UPI: 5–7 business days
  • Razorpay / PayTM: 7–10 business days (subject to payment gateway processing)
  • Cash payments: Refunded in person or via bank transfer within 3 business days

7. Disputes

If you feel your refund request was not handled fairly, please contact us directly at abrar@abrartallks-portfolio.co.in. We will work in good faith to resolve any dispute. All disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of courts in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir, India.
