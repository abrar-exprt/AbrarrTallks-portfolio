What Is MakeMe Top Business Profile (MBP)? Kashmir's #1 Travel Directory Explained

Local SEOAbrar Ali·22 May 2026·6 min read

MakeMe Top Business Profile — The Simple Explanation

MakeMe Top Business Profile — known as MBP — is an online directory created specifically for travel and hospitality businesses in Jammu & Kashmir, India.

Think of it as a verified, Google-indexed phonebook for Kashmir's travel industry. Tourists use it to find genuine travel agencies, resorts, houseboats, tour operators, adventure companies, and camps across J&K. Business owners use it to get a professional online presence and start appearing in Google search results — even if they have no website of their own.

MBP is available at makemetop.in.

What Does "Business Profile" Mean?

A Business Profile on MBP is your business's dedicated page on the MakeMe Top platform. It includes:

  • Your business name, type and category
  • A description of your services and specialties
  • Your location — city, district, area within J&K
  • Your contact details — phone number, WhatsApp, email
  • Your logo and photos of your business
  • A Govt Verified badge if you submit a government registration document

Every profile is indexed on Google with full Schema.org structured data markup — meaning Google understands exactly what your business does and where it is located.

MBP Is a Directory — Not a Booking Platform

This is important to understand clearly. MakeMe Top MBP is a listing directory only.

MBP does not book tours on your behalf. MBP does not collect tourist payments. MBP does not act as a travel agent. When a tourist finds your listing and contacts you, they deal with you directly. MBP simply made it possible for that tourist to find you.

This is a significant advantage for business owners — there is no commission, no percentage fee, and no middleman involved in your bookings. Every enquiry that comes through your MBP listing is 100% yours.

Who Can List on MBP?

Any legitimate travel or hospitality business operating in Jammu & Kashmir, India can apply for an MBP listing:

  • ✈️ Travel Agencies — domestic and international packages from Srinagar, Jammu, Leh
  • 🏨 Resorts and Hotels — luxury stays, budget guesthouses, boutique properties
  • 🚢 Houseboat Owners — Dal Lake, Nagin Lake, Wular Lake, Jhelum houseboats
  • 🗺️ Tour Operators — trekking, sightseeing, pilgrimage and corporate tours
  • ⛷️ Adventure Companies — skiing in Gulmarg, river rafting, paragliding, snow activities
  • 🏕️ Camps and Stays — glamping, forest camps, mountain retreats, eco-stays

Businesses operating across all districts of J&K are welcome — Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, Doodhpathri, Patnitop, Vaishno Devi corridor, Kargil, Leh, and more.

What Is the Govt Verified Badge?

Some MBP listings display a "Govt Verified" badge. This badge appears when a business owner submits a valid government registration document at the time of listing — such as a J&K Tourism Department registration, GST certificate, or FSSAI licence.

The badge signals to tourists that the business has at least taken the step of official registration. For tourists who are cautious about booking with unknown operators — especially those visiting Kashmir for the first time — this badge provides an important layer of trust and confidence.

Note: The Govt Verified badge is informational only. MBP verifies that a document was submitted but does not independently confirm its authenticity or current validity.

How Does the Google Indexing Work?

This is one of MBP's most powerful features for business owners. When your listing is approved, MBP adds it to the website sitemap and applies full Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data to your profile page.

Schema markup is a special code that tells Google exactly what type of business you are, where you are located, what your phone number is, what services you offer, and more. Google uses this data to rank your listing in relevant searches.

The result: tourists searching for "travel agency Srinagar", "houseboat Dal Lake", or "tour operator Pahalgam" can find your MBP listing directly in Google search results — typically within 48 hours of approval.
