lWhy Abrar Ali (Abrarr Tallks) Created MakeMe Top Business Profile for Kashmir

Local SEOAbrar Ali·22 May 2026·6 min read

The Problem Abrar Ali Saw in Kashmir

My name is Abrar Ali — web designer, SEO expert, and founder of rebrar.in, based in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. Most people online know me as Abrarr Tallks.

Over the past four years, I have built websites for dozens of travel businesses across J&K — tour operators, houseboat owners, resorts, travel agencies. And in that time, I noticed the same problem repeating itself with almost every client I worked with:

They were completely invisible on Google.

Kashmir receives millions of tourists every year. These tourists search Google before they travel. They type things like "best houseboat Srinagar", "tour operator Pahalgam", "Kashmir travel agency with Govt registration". And the results they get are dominated by large OTAs — MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Thrillophilia — that take massive commissions from the very businesses I was watching struggle.

The actual local businesses — the genuine houseboat family on Dal Lake, the third-generation travel agency in Lal Chowk, the adventure company in Gulmarg that has been operating for 15 years — were nowhere to be found online.

Why Most J&K Travel Businesses Have No Online Presence

When I started asking business owners why they were not online, the answers were always the same:

  • "A website is too expensive."
  • "I don't understand this SEO thing."
  • "I tried to make a Google profile but I didn't know what to do."
  • "I have a Facebook page but nobody finds me through it."
  • "I depend on referrals from hotels and taxi drivers."

These are real conversations I had with business owners in Srinagar, Pahalgam and Gulmarg. The barrier was not willingness — it was knowledge, time, and cost.

A proper website with SEO costs ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 or more. Then there are hosting fees, maintenance, content writing, and months of SEO work before any results appear. For a small houseboat owner or a family-run travel agency, that is a significant investment with no guaranteed return.

The Idea Behind MakeMe Top Business Profile

I started thinking: what if I could create a single platform that solves this problem for every travel business in J&K at once?

A platform that:

  • Requires no technical knowledge to use
  • Gets businesses found on Google within 48 hours
  • Costs less than ₹800 per month — less than a single local newspaper ad
  • Builds trust with tourists through a Govt Verified badge system
  • Connects tourists directly with businesses — no commissions, no middlemen
  • Focuses exclusively on J&K travel and hospitality — not a generic India-wide directory

That is how MakeMe Top Business Profile was born.

What "MakeMe Top" Actually Means

The name is deliberate. "Make Me Top" — as in, help my business appear at the top of Google search results.

Every local business owner in Kashmir who has ever heard of Google SEO has one goal: get to the top of search results when a tourist searches for their type of business. MBP is built to do exactly that — make your business visible at the top, right where tourists are searching.

The SEO Architecture Behind MBP

As someone who has been doing SEO professionally for four years, I built MBP with a specific technical architecture designed to rank listings in Google:

  • Schema.org LocalBusiness markup on every listing page — Google can read and index the business name, location, category and contact details precisely
  • Sitemap submission — every approved listing is added to the sitemap and submitted to Google Search Console for fast indexing
  • Geo-targeted keyword structure — each listing is optimised for local search terms like "travel agency Srinagar" and "houseboat Dal Lake"
  • Mobile-first design — over 80% of tourist searches for Kashmir travel happen on mobile devices
  • Page speed optimised — fast loading scores that Google rewards in ranking

This is not a generic directory. Every technical decision was made specifically to help J&K travel businesses rank on Google.

Why I Focused Only on J&K Travel Businesses

I deliberately chose not to build a general business directory. There are already hundreds of those in India. None of them rank well for Kashmir-specific travel searches because they are too broad.

By focusing exclusively on Jammu & Kashmir travel and hospitality businesses, MBP becomes the most relevant and authoritative source for exactly those searches. A tourist searching "tour operator Kashmir" is far more likely to find and trust a directory that is entirely about Kashmir travel than a generic India-wide directory that lists everything from restaurants in Mumbai to electricians in Chennai.

Focus creates authority. Authority creates rankings. Rankings bring tourists. Tourists bring bookings.

The Govt Verified Badge — Building Trust for Tourists

One of the most common concerns tourists have about Kashmir travel — particularly first-time visitors — is whether the business they found online is legitimate. There have been cases of tourists being misled by fake or unregistered operators.

The Govt Verified badge addresses this directly. When a business submits a government registration document — J&K Tourism registration, GST certificate, trade licence — their listing receives a visible badge. Tourists can see at a glance which businesses have taken the step of formal registration.

This single feature has made a significant difference in how tourists engage with MBP listings. Verified businesses receive more enquiries.

What MBP Has Achieved So Far

Since launching MakeMe Top Business Profile, the platform has helped travel businesses across Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and other J&K destinations get online and start receiving tourist enquiries through Google.

The pricing model — ₹799/month or ₹7,999/year — was specifically designed so that even the smallest houseboat owner or one-person travel agency can afford a professional online presence. No business in J&K should be invisible on Google because they cannot afford a website.

What Is Next for MBP

MakeMe Top Business Profile is growing. The next major feature being developed is the MBP AI Assistant — an AI-powered tool built into the MBP platform that helps tourists ask questions, get recommendations, and find the right travel business for their specific needs in Kashmir.

The AI will allow tourists to describe what they are looking for — "I want a 3-day family package from Srinagar to Pahalgam with houseboat stay" — and instantly receive matched MBP listings that fit their requirements. This will drive even more targeted enquiries to listed businesses.

A Personal Note

I built MakeMe Top Business Profile because I am from Kashmir. I know how incredible this place is. I know how hard these business owners work. And I know how unfair it is that tourists cannot find them while large OTA platforms take 15–25% commissions from every booking.

MBP is my contribution to making Kashmir's travel industry more accessible, more visible, and more fairly rewarded for the genuine experiences it provides to tourists from across India and the world.
