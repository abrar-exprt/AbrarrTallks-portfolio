How MakeMe Top Business Profile Is Helping Tourism Grow in Jammu & Kashmir

Local SEOAbrar Ali·22 May 2026·6 min read

Kashmir Tourism Is Booming — But Most Businesses Are Invisible

Jammu & Kashmir is one of India's most visited travel destinations. Millions of tourists arrive every year to experience Dal Lake, Gulmarg's ski slopes, Pahalgam's meadows, Sonamarg's glaciers, and the historic streets of Srinagar's old city.

Tourism is the backbone of Kashmir's economy. It supports houseboat families, tour operators, resort owners, local guides, shikara operators, craftsmen, and thousands of families across every district of the valley.

Yet despite this massive tourism activity, the vast majority of local travel businesses in J&K are completely invisible online. They have no website. They do not appear in Google search results. Tourists searching for genuine, local operators cannot find them.

The result: tourists end up booking through large national OTAs — MakeMyTrip, Booking.com, Thrillophilia — that charge 15–25% commission on every booking, pulling money out of local J&K businesses and into corporate platforms.

MakeMe Top Business Profile is working to change this.

The Digital Gap in Kashmir's Travel Industry

Why are so many genuine J&K travel businesses invisible online? There are several reasons:

  • Cost barrier: A proper website with SEO costs ₹10,000–₹25,000 and takes months to rank
  • Knowledge barrier: Most traditional travel business owners in Kashmir do not have digital marketing knowledge
  • Language barrier: Most SEO and web platforms are designed for English-speaking, tech-savvy users
  • Trust barrier: Tourists cannot tell which operators are legitimate and which are not

MBP addresses every single one of these barriers directly.

How MBP Connects Tourists with Real Kashmir Businesses

MakeMe Top Business Profile works as a verified bridge between tourists searching Google and genuine travel businesses operating in Jammu & Kashmir.

Step 1 — Tourists Search Google

A tourist planning a Kashmir trip types a query into Google: "best houseboat Srinagar", "verified tour operator Pahalgam", "ski resort Gulmarg", or "Kashmir family holiday package". These are real searches happening hundreds of times every day.

Step 2 — MBP Listings Appear in Results

MBP listings are Google-indexed with full Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data. This means Google understands each listing precisely — what type of business it is, where it is located, what its phone number is, and what services it offers. MBP listings appear in Google search results for these exact tourist queries.

Step 3 — Tourist Finds a Verified Business

The tourist lands on the MBP listing. They see the business name, description, photos, location, contact details, and — if applicable — the Govt Verified badge. They have all the information they need to make a confident decision.

Step 4 — Direct Contact, No Commission

The tourist contacts the business directly — by phone, WhatsApp, or email. No OTA. No commission. No middleman. The business owner keeps 100% of what they earn from that booking.

Real Impact on J&K Tourism — What MBP Is Changing

Small Businesses Are Getting Found

For the first time, a small houseboat family on Dal Lake or a one-person trekking company in Sonamarg can have a Google-indexed professional presence for ₹799 per month. This was previously impossible without significant investment in website development and SEO.

Tourists Are Finding Genuine Local Operators

Many tourists visiting Kashmir for the first time are nervous about finding trustworthy operators. The MBP Govt Verified badge system helps them identify businesses that have taken the step of formal registration with government authorities — giving them confidence before they book.

More Revenue Stays in Kashmir

When a tourist books directly with a local business through MBP instead of through a national OTA, the entire booking value stays with the local business and within the local economy. This has a direct positive impact on families and communities across J&K who depend on tourism income.

Under-Discovered Destinations Are Getting Exposure

MBP lists businesses not just in Srinagar but across all of J&K — Doodhpathri, Yusmarg, Bangus Valley, Gurez, Bhaderwah, Patnitop, Kishtwar, Vaishno Devi corridor, and more. This helps tourists discover and book experiences in less-visited areas of the valley, spreading tourism benefits more widely.
