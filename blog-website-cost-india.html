How Much Does a Website Cost in India in 2025? (Honest Breakdown)

Pricing Abrar Ali·July 2025·6 min read

One of the most common questions I get: "How much does a website cost?" The honest answer depends on what you need — but there are clear ranges based on project type. I'm Abrar Ali (Abrarr Tallks), CEO of rebrar.in. Here's a transparent breakdown based on real 2025 project prices in India.

Quick Reference: Website Price Ranges in India (2025)

  • Landing Page (1 scroll): ₹4,999 – ₹9,999
  • Portfolio / Personal Site: ₹5,999 – ₹12,999
  • Business Website (5–8 pages): ₹12,999 – ₹24,999
  • Hotel / Travel Booking Site: ₹18,999 – ₹34,999
  • E-Commerce Store: ₹22,999 – ₹45,999
  • SEO Package (monthly): ₹3,999/month

These are real prices for custom-coded, SEO-ready websites — not template builders like Wix or Squarespace.

What Determines the Price?

1 Number of Pages

A single-scroll landing page costs far less than a 10-page multi-section site. More pages = more design, development and SEO work.

2 Design Complexity

A clean minimal layout costs less than a premium animated design with custom illustrations and micro-interactions. Both are valid depending on your brand.

3 Features Required

Standard features (contact form, WhatsApp button, gallery) cost less than custom functionality like booking systems, product catalogues, payment gateways or admin panels.

4 SEO Setup Included

A website with proper on-page SEO, schema markup, geo-tags, sitemap and Google Analytics costs slightly more — but it starts ranking from day one, making the investment worthwhile.

What Must Every Website Price Include?

  • Mobile-responsive design (85%+ Indian users are on mobile)
  • Basic on-page SEO (title tags, meta descriptions, H1 hierarchy)
  • WhatsApp button integration
  • Contact form
  • Domain setup guidance
  • 1–3 months free post-launch support
Warning:

Beware of developers charging ₹1,000–₹2,000 for a "full website". These are usually template sites with no SEO, broken mobile layout and zero support. They cost more in the long run when they don't rank and generate no leads.

Ongoing Costs to Budget For

  • Domain name: ₹500–₹1,200/year (.in or .com)
  • Web hosting: ₹3,000–₹8,000/year, or free on Netlify/Vercel for static sites
  • SSL certificate: Free (Let's Encrypt) or ₹1,000–₹3,000/year
  • Monthly maintenance: ₹1,499/month (optional)

Is a Professional Website Worth It?

A properly built, SEO-ranked website generates leads 24/7. A ₹15,000 investment can generate ₹1,00,000+ in new business over a year if it ranks well and converts visitors. Compare that to Google Ads which costs ₹5,000–₹20,000/month with no guaranteed returns.
