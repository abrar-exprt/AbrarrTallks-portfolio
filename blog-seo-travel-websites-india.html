The 7-Step Travel SEO Process by Abrar Ali

Step 1

Keyword Research for Travel Niches

Finding the exact phrases your potential customers type into Google — "best houseboat in Srinagar", "Kashmir tour package from Mumbai", "Maldives resort for Indian tourists" — with monthly search volume, competition, and ranking difficulty analysis.

Step 2

On-Page SEO Optimization

Optimizing every page — title tags, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, internal links, URL structure — to perfectly match your target keywords and Google's quality guidelines.

Step 3

Schema Markup for Travel Businesses

Adding TouristAttraction, Hotel, TouristTrip, and LocalBusiness structured data so Google displays rich results — star ratings, price ranges, booking links — directly in search results.

Step 4

Geo-Targeting & Local SEO

Setting up Google Business Profile, geo meta tags, and location-specific landing pages to rank in map searches — "tour operator near me in Srinagar", "houseboat Dal Lake".

Step 5

Content Strategy & Blog Posts

Creating SEO-optimized blog content targeting long-tail searches — "best time to visit Kashmir", "how to book a houseboat on Dal Lake" — building topical authority over time.

Step 6

Technical SEO & Core Web Vitals

Ensuring your website loads under 2 seconds on mobile, has proper canonical URLs, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, HTTPS, and passes Google's Core Web Vitals assessment.

Step 7

International SEO for Global Travelers

Implementing hreflang tags, targeting multiple countries with separate landing pages, and building backlinks from international travel directories and tourism websites.
