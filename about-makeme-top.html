About MakeMe Top — India's B2B Business Profile Platform

Business TechAbrar Ali8 min readJune 2, 2026

What Is MakeMe Top?

MakeMe Top Business Profile (MBP) is a B2B SaaS platform built specifically for travel, hospitality, and tourism businesses across India to get discovered on Google — without needing their own website or SEO expertise.

Instead of building a full website (which costs ₹5,000-50,000+), a resort owner, tour operator, or homestay can create a professional Google-ranked business profile on MBP in 15 minutes.

Think of it as a hybrid between a business directory and a SEO tool — but optimized specifically for India's travel industry.

Why MakeMe Top Was Created

During my first 3 years of web design work (rebrar.in), I noticed a consistent pattern:

Small travel businesses couldn't afford websites. A ₹15,000 website was a significant investment for a family-run guesthouse or local tour operator. Most didn't have the budget. Many didn't even know why they needed one.

Yet they were invisible on Google. A tourist searching "resorts near Pahalgam" or "tour operator Srinagar" would find only big OTA players and established chains. The 100+ smaller legitimate businesses in the area simply didn't exist in Google's search results.

The opportunity was obvious: Create an affordable, simple tool that lets small businesses get Google visibility without website building complexity.

MakeMe Top was built to solve this problem. Not as a website builder. Not as a directory. But as a SEO-first business profile platform that ranks on Google.

2. Direct Booking: Message property owners directly, negotiate, and book without paying OTA commissions or dealing with hidden charges.

Core Features That Make MBP Unique

The Verification System

Every business on MBP must submit government verification documents:

  • GST registration (if applicable)
  • Business license / trade certificate
  • Hotel classification / tourism registration
  • Ownership proof

This single feature changes everything. Tourists see a green "Govt Verified" badge and know they're dealing with a legitimate business — not a scam or unregistered operator.

Google-Ranked Business Profiles

Unlike generic directory listings, MBP profiles are:

  • Built for Google's ranking algorithm: Proper schema markup, fast page speeds, mobile-optimized design
  • Rich with high-intent keywords: Each profile naturally includes location, category, amenities — everything tourists search for
  • Authority-building: Profiles are hosted on the MBP domain (high domain authority), giving new businesses credibility boost
  • Continuously indexed: Google crawls MBP regularly, so profile updates rank quickly

AI-Powered Recommendations (MBP AI)

Powered by Firebase and custom machine learning models, MBP's AI learns from tourist search behavior:

"I want a 3-day family package from Delhi to Himachal with adventure activities and good food, under ₹20,000 per person"

Instead of tourists manually filtering 500 listings, the AI instantly shows the 5-10 resorts that actually match their requirements.

Expansion Across All of India

MBP started with Kashmir travel businesses but has expanded because the same problem exists everywhere:

  • Himachal Pradesh: Hundreds of homestays and small resorts invisible on Google
  • Uttarakhand: Hill stations full of quality properties with no digital presence
  • Kerala: Backwater resorts, spice plantation stays, houseboats — many offline
  • Goa: Budget beach huts and heritage stays competing against OTA listings
  • Rajasthan: Desert camps, palace stays, traditional havelis with no Google presence
  • Northeast: Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur — pristine destinations with minimal digital infrastructure
  • Tier-2 Cities: Business travel, wedding venues, conference resorts needing visibility

MBP's expansion across India means all regions now have a local platform where tourism businesses can get Google visibility affordably.

Technical Architecture

MakeMe Top is built on modern, scalable infrastructure:

  • Firebase (Real-time Database): Instant messaging between tourists and businesses, live availability updates, real-time analytics
  • Next.js & React: Fast, SEO-optimized frontend that ranks on Google
  • Google Cloud Platform: Scalable hosting that handles millions of searches and bookings
  • AI/ML Models: Recommendation engine powered by Firebase ML and custom algorithms
  • Google Business API Integration: Direct sync with Google Business Profile for maximum visibility
  • Analytics Dashboard: Custom analytics built with Firebase analytics and Google Analytics 4

MBP AI — The Future

MBP's next major feature is an AI Assistant that acts as a tourist's personal travel advisor:

Current experience: Tourist manually searches 50 listings, filters by price/amenities, contacts 3-4 properties

MBP AI experience: Tourist describes what they want. AI instantly finds the perfect match and facilitates booking.

Built using Firebase ML and custom LLMs, the AI learns from:

  • Which listings tourists actually click and book
  • Seasonal demand patterns (summer beach rush, winter mountain escapes)
  • Price sensitivity by region and traveler type
  • Group composition preferences (family vs. couple vs. solo)

Pricing & ROI

MBP's pricing is simple and affordable:

  • Basic Plan: ₹799/month — Business profile, unlimited inquiries, basic analytics
  • Professional Plan: ₹1,499/month — Everything in Basic + priority support, advanced analytics, seasonal promotions

ROI Example: A resort that gets 1-2 direct bookings per month from MBP (at average ₹10,000 per booking) covers the ₹799 monthly cost and pockets profit. Many MBP-listed businesses report 3-5 inquiries monthly, generating ₹30,000-50,000 in direct revenue.
