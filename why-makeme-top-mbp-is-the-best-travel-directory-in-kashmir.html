Why MakeMe Top MBP Is Different From Other Directories

There are many business directories in India, but none of them are built specifically for travel businesses in Jammu & Kashmir. Here is what makes MBP stand out:

MBP only lists travel-related businesses operating in Jammu & Kashmir. This means when a tourist finds a listing on MBP, they know they are looking at a genuine Kashmir-based travel company — not random businesses from across India. The directory is focused, relevant, and trusted.

2. Google-Indexed Within 48 Hours

Every approved MBP listing is added to the sitemap and marked up with full Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data. This means Google can read your listing properly and index it. Most businesses are visible on Google within 48 hours of approval — a timeline that most travel agencies could never achieve on their own.

3. Government Verified Badge

Businesses that submit a government registration document — such as a J&K Tourism registration or FSSAI certificate — receive a Govt Verified badge on their profile. This badge builds trust with tourists who are looking for legitimate, registered businesses before they book.

MBP is a directory, not a booking platform. When a tourist finds your listing, they contact you directly. No commission. No percentage cut. No middleman taking a share of your booking. You keep 100% of what you earn.

You do not need a website. You do not need to understand SEO. You do not need to know anything about Google. MBP handles all of that for you. Simply fill in your business details, pay, and your listing goes live within 48 hours with full Google indexing built in.
