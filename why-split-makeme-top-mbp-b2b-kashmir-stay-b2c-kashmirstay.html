Why I Split MakeMe Top Into Two Platforms: MakeMe Top for Business Owners and Kashmir Stay for Tourists

Case StudyAbrar Ali·24 May 2026·10 min read

One Platform, Two Audiences — The Problem I Could Not Ignore

My name is Abrar Ali — web designer, SEO expert, founder of rebrar.in and creator of MakeMe Top Business Profile. Most people know me as Abrarr Tallks.

I built MakeMe Top Business Profile (MBP) to solve a specific problem: genuine travel businesses in Jammu & Kashmir were invisible on Google. No website. No listings. No digital presence. While millions of tourists searched for Kashmir travel every month, the real local operators — houseboat families on Dal Lake, trekking companies in Gulmarg, resort owners in Pahalgam — were getting zero of that traffic.

MBP changed that. Business owners could list their business, get Google-indexed within 48 hours, and start receiving direct enquiries from tourists — no OTA commission, no middleman.

But as MBP grew, a problem became impossible to ignore.

I was building one platform that had to speak to two completely different audiences at exactly the same time.

On one side: business owners — travel agency owners, resort managers, houseboat families, tour operators — who needed a professional listing management dashboard, pricing plans, Govt Verified badge submission, analytics, and the ability to update their business profile.

On the other side: tourists — people planning a Kashmir holiday who needed a clean, fast, beautiful interface to browse, discover, filter, and book stays and experiences across J&K.

These two audiences have completely different needs. Completely different design requirements. Completely different language, tone, and journeys through the platform.

Trying to build a single platform that works perfectly for both is like building one app that is simultaneously a professional accounting tool and a travel discovery app. It ends up being mediocre at both.

So I made the decision to split them.

Platform 1 — MakeMe Top (B2B) at makemetop.in

MakeMe Top Business Profile at makemetop.in is now a pure B2B platform — built exclusively for travel and hospitality business owners in Jammu & Kashmir.

B2B means business to business. MakeMe Top's customer is the business owner, not the tourist.

What MakeMe Top B2B Does

  • Listing management dashboard — business owners log in, create and manage their complete business profile: name, description, photos, location, contact details, services, operating season
  • Govt Verified badge — business owners submit government registration documents (J&K Tourism registration, GST, FSSAI) to receive the verified badge on their profile
  • Google SEO indexing — every approved listing is added to the sitemap with full Schema.org LocalBusiness structured data, indexed on Google within 48 hours
  • Spotlight placement — businesses can pay for featured placement at the top of the directory for maximum visibility
  • Analytics — business owners see how many tourists viewed their listing, where the traffic came from, and how many contact interactions occurred
  • Affordable pricing — ₹799/month or ₹7,999/year via Razorpay, designed for small and medium J&K travel businesses

Who MakeMe Top B2B Is For

Any travel or hospitality business operating in J&K:

  • ✈️ Travel agencies and tour operators
  • 🏨 Resorts, hotels and guesthouses
  • 🚢 Houseboat owners on Dal Lake, Nagin Lake, Wular Lake
  • ⛷️ Adventure companies — skiing, trekking, rafting, paragliding
  • 🏕️ Camp and eco-stay operators
  • 🙏 Pilgrimage tour providers — Vaishno Devi, Amarnath corridors

The B2B Focus Makes MakeMe Top Stronger

By removing the tourist-facing booking experience from MakeMe Top, the platform can now be designed entirely around what business owners need. The dashboard is cleaner. The onboarding is faster. The listing management tools are more powerful. The SEO architecture can be refined specifically for business-to-directory indexing.

Business owners no longer land on a homepage that is half trying to sell them a listing and half trying to help tourists find their hotel. They land on a platform that is 100% built for them.

Platform 2 — Kashmir Stay (B2C) at kashmirstay.in

Kashmir Stay at kashmirstay.in is the new pure B2C platform — built exclusively for tourists who are planning a trip to Jammu & Kashmir and need to find the right stay or experience.

B2C means business to consumer. Kashmir Stay's customer is the tourist.

What Kashmir Stay B2C Does

  • Beautiful, fast discovery experience — a clean, image-led interface designed for tourists browsing stays and experiences across J&K on their phone
  • Smart filtering — filter by location (Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg, Leh), type of stay (houseboat, resort, camp, guesthouse), budget, group size, and season
  • Verified listings only — every business visible on Kashmir Stay is an MBP-verified listing, so tourists only see legitimate, reviewed businesses
  • Direct contact buttons — call, WhatsApp, or email the business directly from the listing page — no OTA, no commission, real prices
  • Kashmir Stay AI — an AI-powered assistant built into kashmirstay.in that lets tourists describe exactly what they want and instantly receive matched recommendations from the verified listing database
  • Destination guides — content about each major J&K destination: what to expect, best season, travel tips, local insights — helping tourists make more confident decisions

Why Tourists Needed a Separate Platform

When a tourist lands on a website to find a Kashmir holiday, they do not care about listing pricing plans, badge verification processes, or dashboard analytics. They want to see beautiful photos of Dal Lake houseboats. They want to quickly filter resorts by location and price. They want to read about what Pahalgam is like in July. They want an AI to tell them which camp is best for a family of four in June.

Everything about Kashmir Stay — the design, the copy, the navigation, the AI, the content — is built around the tourist's journey, not the business owner's journey. That separation produces a dramatically better experience for tourists, which means more enquiries for the businesses listed on MBP.

The Kashmir Stay AI — Personalised Travel Recommendations

The most powerful feature of Kashmir Stay B2C is the Kashmir Stay AI.

A tourist opens kashmirstay.in, taps the AI assistant, and types:

"I want a 5-night honeymoon stay in Kashmir in September — houseboat on Dal Lake for 2 nights, then a mountain resort in Pahalgam or Sonamarg. Budget ₹8,000 per night. Vegetarian food important."

Kashmir Stay AI reads the full request — destination, duration, type of stay, group size, budget, dietary requirement — and instantly matches it against the entire verified MBP listing database. It returns the most relevant options from verified businesses, ranked by relevance to the tourist's exact requirements.

No generic search results. No irrelevant listings. No need to browse 40 pages manually.

This is the core of why splitting the platforms unlocks something that was impossible in the merged version — the AI on the tourist platform can be trained and optimised entirely for tourist discovery, while the B2B platform can focus on building the database quality that makes the AI useful.

How the Two Platforms Work Together

MakeMe Top and Kashmir Stay are separate platforms — but they are powered by the same verified listing database.

Here is the full flow:

  • Step 1: A business owner visits makemetop.in, creates a listing, submits verification documents, pays via Razorpay, and gets approved within 48 hours
  • Step 2: The approved listing is indexed on Google with full Schema.org markup — business now appears in Google search results
  • Step 3: The same approved listing automatically appears on kashmirstay.in in the tourist-facing discovery interface
  • Step 4: Kashmir Stay AI uses the verified listing database to power personalised recommendations for tourists
  • Step 5: A tourist finds the business through Kashmir Stay AI, Google search, or direct browsing — and contacts them directly
  • Step 6: The business receives a direct enquiry. No commission. 100% of the booking value stays with the business

One listing. Two platforms. Maximum reach.

Why This Is Better for Everyone

Better for Business Owners

Business owners get a professional, focused B2B tool at makemetop.in — not a platform that is half-trying to talk to tourists. Their listing automatically powers tourist discovery on kashmirstay.in without any additional work. One payment. One listing. Presence on both platforms.

Better for Tourists

Tourists get a beautiful, fast, AI-powered discovery platform at kashmirstay.in built entirely around their needs. Every listing they see is verified through MBP. They can find their perfect Kashmir stay in minutes instead of hours. And they contact businesses directly — getting real prices without commission markup.

Better for Kashmir Tourism

The split creates a more professional, more scalable ecosystem for Kashmir's tourism industry. MakeMe Top becomes the trusted, authoritative B2B registry for J&K travel businesses. Kashmir Stay becomes the go-to tourist discovery platform for anyone planning a Kashmir trip. Both platforms reinforce each other and both contribute to keeping tourist spending within the local Kashmir economy.

The Bigger Picture — Why This Matters for J&K

Kashmir's tourism industry is at an inflection point. Tourist arrivals have been growing consistently year after year. The infrastructure — airports, roads, connectivity — is improving. International awareness of Kashmir as a premium travel destination is growing.

But the digital infrastructure for Kashmir's tourism industry has not kept pace. Most of the digital tourism economy for J&K is still controlled by large national OTAs that extract significant commission from every booking. Local businesses are the product — but they receive a fraction of the value.

MakeMe Top (B2B) and Kashmir Stay (B2C) together are my attempt to build a locally-owned digital infrastructure for Kashmir's tourism economy. A system where local businesses control their own listings. Where tourists can find genuine local operators. Where technology — including AI — serves the J&K tourism ecosystem rather than extracting from it.

This is a long-term project. The split into two focused platforms is not the end — it is the beginning of what each platform can become when it is built with a single audience in mind.

Important Disclaimer

Both MakeMe Top at makemetop.in and Kashmir Stay at kashmirstay.in are listing and discovery platforms only. Neither platform books tours, collects tourist payments, or takes responsibility for any service, experience, or transaction involving a listed business. Tourists contact and transact with businesses entirely at their own risk. Always verify business details before making any payment. Read the full Terms & Conditions before using either platform.
